The Vitruvian philosophy

Care that sees the whole person.

Movement is individual. Your treatment should be too. We bring together hands-on care, rehabilitation and modern therapeutic techniques to support your physical wellbeing.

A more considered approach to physiotherapy

Your body has its own story. Your care should reflect it.

At Vitruvian Italian Physiotherapy Center, the focus is on understanding how your body moves, what is limiting you and what you want to return to doing. Treatment is selected around your needs rather than a one-size-fits-all routine.

With Italian roots and a movement-focused philosophy, the clinic combines manual treatment approaches, exercise-based rehabilitation and therapeutic technologies to help patients work towards meaningful improvements in everyday function.

Treatment options

Different techniques. One individual plan.

Your physiotherapist can recommend suitable techniques based on your assessment, symptoms and goals. Treatment options available at the clinic include:

Manual Therapy

Hands-on techniques including joint manipulation, soft-tissue approaches and other manual treatments.

Exercise Therapy

Guided movement and strengthening exercises to support mobility, control and functional goals.

TECAR Therapy

A therapeutic energy-based modality offered as part of selected physiotherapy treatment plans.

Shockwave Therapy

A treatment modality that may be considered for selected musculoskeletal conditions.

Dry Needling

A targeted technique that may be used by a qualified practitioner where clinically appropriate.

Specialist Care

Options including lymphatic drainage, postural education, kinesiotaping and other specialised services.
